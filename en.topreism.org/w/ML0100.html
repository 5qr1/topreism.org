ML0100

101-Key ANSI Realforce R1 Keyboard.

DESCRIPTION

The Realforce ML0100 (marketing name Realforce 101) is a standard fullsize 101-Key Realforce. It was produced starting in April of 2003, and was Realforce's first ANSI keyboard. It is 1 of 3 known WKL ANSI R1 Realforces. As is standard from boards this early, it uses the old blue Realforce logo on the locklight, uses a PS/2 connector, variable weight domes, and lacks dipswitches. It was unfortunately discontinued in August of 2012 along with several other early fullsize Realforce keyboards.
