TOPREISM WIKI
=============

This wiki is maintained and written mainly by 1 person. No AI will ever be knowingly used in any capacity to produce or assist in producing any aspect of this wiki.

Please be aware that, as of writing this, the wiki is extremely incomplete, and it can never be truly considered complete. There are certainly hundreds of topre keyboard models unknown to us and perhaps thousands waiting to be released.

Wiki pages are styled similarly to UNIX manpages for ease of reading and writing them. :- )

This wiki assumes basic knowledge of keyboards and terms used to describe them. For the uninitiated, you can read about the <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topre|topre> keyboard switch <topre.html|here>, and you can learn how to contribute <contrib.html|here>.
