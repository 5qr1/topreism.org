LA0100

106-Key JIS Realforce R1 Keyboard.

DESCRIPTION

The Realforce LA0100 (marketing name Realforce 106) is a standard fullsize WKL 106-Key JIS Realforce. The earliest Realforce model, production began in June of 2001. As with many early Realforce R1s, the LA0100 uses variable weight domes, a PS/2 connector, and the old blue Realforce logo on the locklight badge. Notably, very early examples of the Realforce 106 had OADG109 legends. Every other Japanese Realforce uses OADG109A legends, making it the only Realforce to use the preceding standard. Production ended in August of 2012.
